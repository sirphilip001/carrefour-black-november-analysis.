# Where Are the Best Black November Deals at Carrefour Kenya?

**Source and scope:** Carrefour Kenya online promotions snapshot, 11 November 2026; 370 de-duplicated product listings.

![Four-chart dashboard of Carrefour Black November deals](../outputs/figures/deals_dashboard.png)

## Findings

Food & Beverages accounted for the largest share of listed products, with 109 products (29.5%). Fashion & Luggage had the highest average actual discount by department, and its Express products averaged 47.1%; the most common discount interval overall was 10.6%–15.3% (122 products). The largest cash saving was KES 26,502 on the Von Fridge VARZ-20NHK, showing that the biggest percentage discounts and the largest cash savings point to different deals in this snapshot.
